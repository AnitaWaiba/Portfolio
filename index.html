About me

I'm an IT graduate with a passion for creating meaningful digital experiences through thoughtful design and technology.

I enjoy exploring the intersection of creativity and functionality, particularly in UI/UX design and web development. From designing intuitive e-commerce interfaces to developing web-based platforms, I love turning ideas into experiences that are both visually engaging and easy to use.

I'm naturally curious, detail-oriented, and always eager to learn something new. I believe good design goes beyond appearance — it's about understanding people, solving problems, and making everyday interactions simpler.

Currently, I'm focused on expanding my skills, exploring new ideas, and growing as a designer and developer.
